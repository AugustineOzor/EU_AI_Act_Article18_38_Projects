# AI Incident Classification Matrix

| Level | Internal description | Example | Required response |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Minor control exception | Incomplete metadata with no decision impact | Correct through normal workflow |
| 2 | Material issue | Localized performance drop | Owner escalation and planned remediation |
| 3 | Significant risk | Possible discriminatory outcome | Restrict use, executive/legal review |
| 4 | Severe issue | Widespread harm, security compromise or unlawful use | Suspend, consider withdrawal/recall and regulatory escalation |

These are internal portfolio categories, not statutory legal categories.
