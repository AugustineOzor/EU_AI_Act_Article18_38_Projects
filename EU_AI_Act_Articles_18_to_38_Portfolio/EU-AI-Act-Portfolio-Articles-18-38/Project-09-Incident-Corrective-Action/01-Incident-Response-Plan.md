# AI Incident Response Plan

## Workflow

1. Detect and register the event.
2. Validate scope and severity.
3. Preserve evidence.
4. Contain the affected model, feature, data source or deployment.
5. Escalate to legal, compliance, security, quality and business owners.
6. Investigate root cause and affected decisions.
7. Select corrective and preventive actions.
8. Notify relevant actors and authorities where required.
9. Retest and independently validate.
10. Approve restart and monitor enhanced indicators.

## Immediate containment options

- Disable automated recommendations
- Restrict an affected job family
- Roll back the model or configuration
- Suspend data ingestion
- Require enhanced manual review
- Stop distribution or use
