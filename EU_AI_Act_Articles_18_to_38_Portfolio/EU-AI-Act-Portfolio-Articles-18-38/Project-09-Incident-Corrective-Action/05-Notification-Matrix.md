# Incident Notification Matrix

| Recipient | Trigger | Owner | Required content | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deployers | Material product issue affecting use | Customer Compliance Lead | Issue, affected versions, containment, action | Secure customer notice |
| Authorized representative | Relevant EU compliance issue | Regulatory Affairs | Facts, actions and evidence | Mandated channel |
| Importers/distributors | Market action or stop-distribution need | Supply-Chain Lead | Affected products and instructions | Contract contact tree |
| Authority/notified body | When legally required | Legal/Regulatory Affairs | Validated facts and corrective action | Official secure route |
