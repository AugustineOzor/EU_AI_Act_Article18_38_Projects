# AI Incident Tabletop Exercise Report

## Scenario

Post-market monitoring identifies a potential discriminatory recommendation pattern affecting two customers and one model version.

## Objectives

- Test escalation and decision rights.
- Confirm evidence preservation.
- Test customer and authority notification readiness.
- Validate rollback and restart approval.

## Findings

- Technical rollback was available.
- Customer contact details were current.
- Deployer log export was inconsistent.
- Authority-response roles required clearer deputies.

## Improvement actions

Update contracts, maintain a deputy contact roster, standardize evidence export and rerun the exercise.
