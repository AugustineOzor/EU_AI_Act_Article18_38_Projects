# Article 25 Responsibility-Shift Assessment

## Change proposal

A distributor rebrands TalentMatch AI, changes scoring weights and markets the modified system under its own trademark.

## Assessment questions

- Is the system placed under the distributor’s own name or trademark?
- Is the modification substantial?
- Does the intended purpose change?
- Does the system remain or become high-risk?
- Who controls documentation, testing and conformity decisions?

## Portfolio conclusion

The proposal requires formal legal review and should be treated as a potential provider-role shift. Market activity is paused until responsibilities and conformity obligations are resolved.
