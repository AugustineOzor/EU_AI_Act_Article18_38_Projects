# Authorized Representative Mandate Template

## Mandate scope

The provider authorizes the representative to perform specified EU AI Act tasks, including record availability, authority cooperation and registration support where applicable.

## Required clauses

- Parties and AI systems covered
- Effective date and duration
- Exact delegated tasks
- Record custody and access
- Authority communications
- Incident and corrective-action cooperation
- Confidentiality and security
- Change notification
- Mandate termination and authority notification
- Return or transfer of records

The provider remains responsible for obligations that cannot be transferred by contract.
