# Importer and Distributor Verification Checklists

## Importer

- [ ] Provider and system identity verified
- [ ] Conformity assessment completed
- [ ] Technical documentation available
- [ ] Declaration of conformity available
- [ ] CE marking verified where required
- [ ] Authorized representative confirmed where required
- [ ] No reason to believe the system is non-compliant
- [ ] Storage and transport controls defined

## Distributor

- [ ] Required marking present
- [ ] Declaration and instructions available
- [ ] Provider/importer identification present
- [ ] Stop-distribution trigger defined
- [ ] Complaint, withdrawal and recall route tested
