# Third-Party AI Contract Requirements

- AI system, component, model and service scope
- Intended and prohibited uses
- Provider/deployer and value-chain responsibilities
- Documentation and evidence delivery
- Data use, training, retention and deletion
- Model and feature change notification
- Security, logging and incident support
- Audit and assessment cooperation
- Authority and notified-body cooperation
- Subcontractor controls
- Intellectual property and licensing
- Exit, record transfer, suspension, withdrawal and recall support
