# AI Supply-Chain Obligations Matrix

| Actor | Core responsibility | Evidence | Escalation trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Provider | System compliance and provider obligations | Technical file, QMS, conformity evidence | Non-compliance or material change |
| Authorized representative | Mandated EU contact and records | Written mandate, record index | Provider non-compliance |
| Importer | Pre-market verification | Import verification checklist | Missing marking or documentation |
| Distributor | Pre-distribution checks and market action | Distribution checklist | Reason to believe non-compliance |
| Deployer | Responsible use and oversight | Operating records and FRIA where applicable | Incident, misuse or control failure |
