# Contestability and Complaint Procedure

1. Provide a clear contact route.
2. Verify the applicant and identify the relevant decision.
3. Preserve the decision record and model/version evidence.
4. Assign review to trained personnel not solely reliant on the original output.
5. Correct inaccurate information.
6. Reconsider the outcome where appropriate.
7. Communicate the result in understandable language.
8. Track themes and feed them into monitoring and risk review.
