# Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment

## System and purpose

TalentMatch AI supports recruitment shortlisting. Final decisions remain with trained recruiters.

## Affected people

External and internal applicants, including people whose career paths or qualifications may be represented differently in data.

## Potential impacts

- Unequal access to employment
- Discrimination or proxy discrimination
- Privacy and data-protection impacts
- Lack of explanation or meaningful challenge
- Automation bias
- Accessibility barriers

## Safeguards

- Mandatory human review
- Bias and performance testing
- Data-quality controls
- Clear applicant notice
- Correction and complaint route
- Monitoring of outcomes and overrides
- Pause and escalation authority

## Residual assessment

Conditional deployment only. Material changes, significant complaints, or threshold breaches require reassessment and governance approval.
