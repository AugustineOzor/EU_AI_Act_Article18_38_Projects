# Deployer Monitoring Plan

| Indicator | Frequency | Threshold response | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Human review completion | Weekly | Block unreviewed decisions | HR Operations |
| Override patterns | Monthly | Investigate unusual concentration or absence | AI Governance |
| Input-data defects | Weekly | Correct source and assess affected cases | Data Owner |
| Complaints and challenges | Monthly | Trend review and risk escalation | Compliance |
| Outcome differences | Monthly during pilot | Independent analysis and possible pause | Validator |
| Log availability | Quarterly | Contract and technical remediation | Security |
