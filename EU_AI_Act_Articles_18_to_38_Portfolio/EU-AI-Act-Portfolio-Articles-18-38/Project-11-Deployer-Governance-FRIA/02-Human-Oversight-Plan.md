# Human Oversight Plan

## Oversight model

Human-in-the-loop: a trained recruiter must review each recommendation before a candidate is advanced or removed.

## Authority

Oversight personnel may disregard, correct, override, escalate, pause use and request investigation.

## Competence

Training covers intended purpose, limitations, automation bias, explanation, data issues, override, incident reporting and applicant challenge.

## Evidence

Review timestamps, corrections, overrides, reasons, escalations, pause events and training records are retained.
