# Subcontractor and Subsidiary Governance Procedure

- Confirm provider agreement where required.
- Verify Article 31-equivalent competence, independence and confidentiality.
- Define the delegated scope and prohibited decisions.
- Notify the relevant authority as required.
- Supervise work, review evidence and retain full accountability.
- Retain qualification and work records for the applicable period.
- Reassess after material staff, scope or ownership changes.
