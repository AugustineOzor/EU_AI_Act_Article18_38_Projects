# Independence, Impartiality and Conflict Framework

## Prohibited conflicts

- Designing, developing or marketing the assessed system
- Consulting on controls being independently assessed
- Financial interests in the provider or system
- Assessment by personnel with recent conflicting relationships

## Process

Declare -> screen -> evaluate -> mitigate -> approve or remove -> monitor -> retain evidence.

A standing impartiality committee reviews systemic conflicts, commercial pressure and high-risk engagements.
