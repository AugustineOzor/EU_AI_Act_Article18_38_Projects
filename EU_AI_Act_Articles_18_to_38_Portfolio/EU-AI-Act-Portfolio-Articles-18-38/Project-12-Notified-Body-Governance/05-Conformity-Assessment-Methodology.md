# Conformity Assessment Methodology

1. Confirm application, scope and conflicts.
2. Plan competence, sampling and assessment activities.
3. Review QMS and technical documentation.
4. Evaluate risk management, data governance, testing, logging, transparency, oversight, accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity.
5. Review nonconformities and corrective action.
6. Conduct independent technical review.
7. Make a certification decision separate from assessment delivery.
8. Define surveillance and change-notification conditions.

Proportionality may simplify administration but must not lower the required protection or evidence standard.
