# Regulatory Information Request Procedure

1. Validate the authority, request, scope, deadline and confidentiality channel.
2. Appoint a response lead and legal reviewer.
3. Preserve relevant evidence and suspend routine disposal where necessary.
4. Map every request item to the evidence index.
5. Review records for accuracy, completeness, privilege, confidentiality and trade-secret handling.
6. Approve the response through accountable management.
7. Transmit through the approved secure channel.
8. Record questions, supplemental information and closure.
9. Conduct lessons learned and correct evidence gaps.

## Response checklist

- [ ] Request logged
- [ ] Authority verified
- [ ] Evidence preserved
- [ ] Data and logs retrieved
- [ ] Legal review complete
- [ ] Management approval complete
- [ ] Secure transmission confirmed
- [ ] Final response archived
