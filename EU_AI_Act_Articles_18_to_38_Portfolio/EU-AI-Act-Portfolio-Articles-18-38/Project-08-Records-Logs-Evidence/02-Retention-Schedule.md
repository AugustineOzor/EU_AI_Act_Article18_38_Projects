# AI Records Retention Schedule

| Record category | Baseline rule | Trigger | Owner | Disposal approval | Special controls |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Article 18 technical and QMS documentation | Retain for the applicable statutory period | System placed on market or put into service | Compliance | Legal and records manager | Legal hold, immutable version |
| Automatically generated logs under provider control | Retain for the applicable Article 19 period or other applicable law | Log event date | Security | Security and privacy | Minimize personal data |
| Incident and CAPA records | Organizational/legal schedule | Incident closure | Quality | Legal and quality | Preserve investigation evidence |
| Supplier assurance records | Contract plus legal schedule | Contract termination | Procurement | Legal and procurement | Preserve audit and change notices |

## Exceptions

A retention exception requires business justification, privacy review, legal approval, compensating controls, an expiry date, and documented closure.
