# Certificate Continuity Review

| Certificate group | Risk from change | Immediate action | Continuity decision | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recruitment AI certificates | Loss of lead competence | Independent file review | Continue subject to successful review | Review report |
| Open assessments | Capacity reduction | Pause and transfer/replace assessor | Case-by-case | Transfer records |
| Certificates with unresolved findings | Elevated | Enhanced surveillance | Conditional | CAPA evidence |
