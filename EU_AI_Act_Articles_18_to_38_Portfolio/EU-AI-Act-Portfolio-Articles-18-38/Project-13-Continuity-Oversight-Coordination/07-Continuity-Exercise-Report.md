# Continuity Exercise Report

## Scenario

A loss of accreditation and key staff requires immediate suspension of part of the notification scope.

## Results

- Open-case inventory completed.
- Secure records transfer process worked.
- Certificate-review criteria were available.
- Provider communications required a clearer single point of contact.
- Coordination group notification was not included in the first draft plan.

## Actions

Update contacts, add coordination obligations, test certificate sampling and repeat the exercise annually or after material change.
