# Competence Challenge Response Plan

## Response team

Executive lead, legal, quality, technical competence, security and records owners.

## Steps

- Validate and log the request.
- Preserve relevant records.
- Produce competence, independence, quality and assessment evidence.
- Cooperate with notifying authority and Commission processes.
- Address confidentiality and trade secrets.
- Implement restrictions or corrective action where needed.
- Track decisions, appeal/response rights and closure.
