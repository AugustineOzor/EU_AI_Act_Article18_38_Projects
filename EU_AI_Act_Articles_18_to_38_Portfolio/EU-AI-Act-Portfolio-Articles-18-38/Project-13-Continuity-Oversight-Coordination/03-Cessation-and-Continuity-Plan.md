# Cessation and Continuity Plan

- Notify the authority and affected providers.
- Stop accepting new work.
- Preserve confidentiality, records and evidence integrity.
- Inventory open assessments and issued certificates.
- Identify a qualified successor where appropriate.
- Transfer files securely with authorization and chain of custody.
- Review certificate validity and needed action.
- Maintain regulatory access to records.
- Communicate closure and complete lessons learned.
