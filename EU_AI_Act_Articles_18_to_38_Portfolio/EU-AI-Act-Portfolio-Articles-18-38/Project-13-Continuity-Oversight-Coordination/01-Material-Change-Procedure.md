# Material Change Procedure

1. Identify the change.
2. Assess impact on competence, independence, resources, scope, security and open work.
3. Classify as minor, material or cessation-related.
4. Notify the appropriate authority where required.
5. Restrict affected activities if capability is uncertain.
6. Review open assessments and issued certificates.
7. Implement corrective action.
8. Approve closure and retain evidence.

Triggers include ownership, accreditation, key staff, scope, systems, subcontractors, security incidents and financial viability.
