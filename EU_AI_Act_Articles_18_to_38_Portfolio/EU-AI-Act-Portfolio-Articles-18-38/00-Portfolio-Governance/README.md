# EU AI Act Portfolio Programme: Articles 18-38

> **Portfolio disclaimer:** This project uses fictional organizations, systems, events, findings, and evidence. It demonstrates governance methodology only. It is not legal advice, accreditation, certification, or a formal conformity assessment. Confirm requirements against the current official EU AI Act text, applicable annexes, harmonised standards, guidance, contracts, and competent legal advice.


## Programme purpose

This portfolio demonstrates how regulatory requirements can be translated into practical AI governance, risk, compliance, assurance, records, incident, supply-chain, deployer, and conformity-assessment controls.

## Fictional case environment

**Provider:** Meridian Digital Services  
**High-risk AI system:** TalentMatch AI  
**Intended purpose:** Support recruitment teams by extracting qualifications, matching candidates to documented job requirements, creating candidate rankings, and providing recruiter-facing explanations.  
**Human oversight:** A recruiter must review recommendations before a candidate is advanced or removed.  
**Deployment model:** Meridian supplies the system to European customers. Meridian acts as provider; customers act as deployers. External cloud, model, data, testing, importer, distributor, and representative relationships exist.
